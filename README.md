# Argent Research: all-weather risk management

Live site: https://peterhanck.github.io/argent-research/

A working MVP. Describe a business risk or upload a ledger, and the page sizes a Kalshi hedge, prices it against a
snapshot of the order book, and shows your cash whether the bet comes right or wrong. Everything runs in the browser;
the worked examples use fictional businesses. This site doesn't hold money, place trades or give investment advice, and
it is not affiliated with Kalshi.
