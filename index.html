Case study

How a cross-border seller hedges its next twelve months.

Nimbus Home Goods imports kitchenware and lamps from China and sells on Amazon, Shopify and TikTok. Argent read five years of its books and built a plan for the year ahead.

How it works

Three steps. Both worlds.

  1. 01

    Say what could hurt

    One sentence with a number, or your bookkeeping export. From books, Argent finds the exposures itself: duties, freight, fuel, rates, currency.

  2. 02

    Price the closest contract

    Argent reads the Kalshi strike ladder, sizes contracts to shrink the swing in your year under each way your books say you might act, and walks the live order book for the real cost and fees.

  3. 03

    See right and wrong

    What you pay today, what comes back if the event lands, your cash if it doesn't, and with books, your whole year with and without the hedge.
