    How it works

    Connect. Analyze. Hedge.

    1. 01

      Connect

      Add a transaction export and, if you have one, a 12-month forecast. Or link your bank. Argent reads categories, counterparties and currencies.

    2. 02

      Analyze

      Argent ties each source of cash to the price, rate or demand that moves it, then measures how much each risk could swing your next 12 months, taking the odds from Kalshi's own prices and exchange-rate history.

    3. 03

      Hedge

      It shows how much of each risk Kalshi contracts and currency forwards can offset, sizes one plan across both, and projects your year with and without it.

    Demo

    Link your bank

    This demo has no live bank connection. To show the flow, it loads a sample bank feed instead: five years of transactions from Nimbus Home Goods, a fictional importer.

    • A live link would share your transactions read-only, through Plaid.
    • Argent would never see your bank login.

    Argent Research, all-weather risk management. This MVP doesn't hold money, place trades or give investment advice; any order happens on Kalshi, and any forward with your bank or payment provider. Not affiliated with Kalshi or Plaid.
